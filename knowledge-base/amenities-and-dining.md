# Amenities and Dining

## Room types
- **Sea View** rooms face the ocean with a private balcony.
- **Garden View** rooms overlook the landscaped gardens and are pet-friendly.

## Hotel amenities
- Complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi throughout the property.
- Outdoor swimming pool, open 7:00 AM to 9:00 PM.
- Fitness centre, open 24 hours.
- Full-service spa (advance booking recommended).
- Business centre and meeting rooms.
- On-site parking; valet parking available for an additional fee.

## Dining
- **The Terrace** – all-day dining, breakfast buffet 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM.
- **Horizon Rooftop Bar** – evening drinks and light bites, 5:00 PM to midnight.
- **In-room dining** – available 24 hours.
- Dietary requirements (vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, allergies) can be
  accommodated with advance notice.

## Accessibility
- Accessible rooms and step-free routes to all public areas are available.
- Please request accessibility features at the time of booking.
