# Frequently Asked Questions

**What time is check-in and check-out?**
Check-in is from 3:00 PM and check-out is by 11:00 AM.

**Do you offer free Wi-Fi?**
Yes, complimentary high-speed Wi-Fi is available throughout the hotel.

**Is parking available?**
Yes. On-site parking is available, and valet parking can be arranged for an
additional fee.

**Are pets allowed?**
Pets are welcome in Garden View rooms (one pet per room, cleaning fee applies).
Service animals are always welcome at no charge.

**What room types do you offer?**
Sea View rooms with ocean-facing balconies, and pet-friendly Garden View rooms
overlooking the gardens.

**Can I cancel my booking?**
Yes, free of charge up to 48 hours before check-in. Within 48 hours, the first
night is charged.

**Is breakfast included?**
Breakfast is served at The Terrace from 7:00 AM to 10:30 AM. Whether it is
included depends on your rate; please check at booking.

**Do you have a pool and gym?**
Yes, an outdoor pool (7:00 AM–9:00 PM) and a 24-hour fitness centre.
