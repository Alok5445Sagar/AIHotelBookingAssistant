# Hotel Policies

## Check-in and check-out
- Check-in time is from 3:00 PM (15:00) local time.
- Check-out time is by 11:00 AM (11:00) local time.
- Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and may incur a fee.

## Cancellation policy
- Bookings may be cancelled free of charge up to 48 hours before the check-in date.
- Cancellations within 48 hours of check-in are charged for the first night.
- No-shows are charged for the first night.

## Payment
- A valid credit or debit card is required to guarantee a reservation.
- Accepted methods: major credit cards and debit cards.

## Identification
- All guests must present a valid government-issued photo ID at check-in.
- The minimum age to check in is 18 years.

## Pets
- Pets are welcome in Garden View rooms only, with a maximum of one pet per room.
- A cleaning fee applies per stay. Service animals are always welcome at no charge.

## Smoking
- All rooms and indoor areas are non-smoking.
- Designated outdoor smoking areas are available.
